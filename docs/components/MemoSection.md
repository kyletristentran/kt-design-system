# MemoSection

A numbered section head with an 11pt title over a hairline rule, followed by full-measure prose.

## Consumer provides
- `h2.kt-memo-section` with a two-digit `.kt-memo-section__n` and the heading text, then paragraphs and optional bullets.

## Rules
- Each heading states the claim the section defends, in plain words. No epigrams or imperatives.
- No paragraph runs past five lines.
- End on the risks or the ask. There is no Conclusion section that repeats the BLUF.
